# Transfer findings

The coarse rubric scored all 12 pairs equally at 10/10. The blind judge nevertheless preferred the skill-assisted answer in two cases and tied the other ten. It preferred the concrete £8,000 evaluation-budget breakdown in the Mill case and a stronger comparative trial design in the council case. Those preferences are recorded separately from score wins; reporting “two score improvements” would be inaccurate.

These tasks were deliberately more complex than the initial examples, with constraints, competing priorities, and imperfect data. The baseline remained very strong. The pilot does not show that a philosophical lens generally improves a capable agent's performance. It also cannot distinguish a philosophy-specific benefit from additional structured instructions.

No skill changes were made before this suite. A later source-review correction to the Rawls wording creates version 0.1.1 and is checked separately. Preserve the original answers, hashes, and scores instead of relabeling them as evaluations of the revised release.
