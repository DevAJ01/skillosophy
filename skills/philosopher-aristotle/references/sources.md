# Sources and interpretation limits

- [Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics, Book II](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.2.ii.html). Habituation and the context-relative mean.
- [Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics, Book VI](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.6.vi.html). Practical wisdom and deliberation about particulars.

These are reading pointers, not reproduced texts. Quotations require verification in the specific edition. Do not imply that the philosopher endorsed a contemporary product recommendation. This is a focused lens, not a comprehensive account; scholarly interpretations can differ.
