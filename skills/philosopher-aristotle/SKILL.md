# Aristotelian Practical Wisdom

Connect a practice to the good it is supposed to achieve and the capacities it cultivates. This adapts Aristotle's ethics while rejecting the automatic transfer of ancient social hierarchies to contemporary work.

## Method

Identify the practice's purpose and ask whose flourishing it serves. Distinguish an instrument or proxy from the activity's worthwhile end. Success at a metric can undermine the good the practice exists to deliver.

Identify the relevant excellence: honesty, courage, fairness, generosity, or another capacity. Describe concrete deficient and excessive responses in this context. The mean is what practical judgment finds appropriate to circumstances, not the arithmetic midpoint, and some actions should not be reframed as acceptable in moderation.

Attend to particulars: roles, relationships, timing, available knowledge, and consequences for the people involved. A virtuous label is not a substitute for judging the case. Consider what a competent and responsible practitioner would notice that a mechanical rule misses.

Turn the desired quality into repeated practice with feedback. Inspect incentives and resource constraints so a failure of the system is not reduced to a defect in individual character. Specify an opportunity to practice, a sign of improvement, and a review that can correct a bad habit.

Recommend conduct or a process that supports the purpose over time. Explain how the habit serves the end and where discretion needs a boundary. Do not infer a natural purpose for a modern institution without defending it.

## Useful output

State the purpose, context-sensitive excellence, competing errors, and a concrete practice with feedback. Where the user needs wording or a policy, produce it.

## Worked application — original example

Request: “How should a new manager handle an underperforming teammate?”
Avoid both evading a necessary conversation and humiliating the person. Give specific evidence, ask about obstacles, agree on support and expectations, and revisit progress at a defined time. The practice cultivates honest, fair judgment; success is improved work and understanding, not the mere fact a difficult conversation occurred.

## Failure checks

Have you mistaken balance for splitting the difference? Have you moralized a capacity problem? Is the proposed habit aligned with the purpose rather than its proxy?

## Boundaries

Use this as a reasoning lens, not a simulation of the philosopher or proof that a conclusion is correct. Follow the user's requested deliverable and constraints. Give concise reasons, assumptions, and decision criteria rather than a private deliberation transcript. Do not invent quotations, sources, historical positions, motives, or numerical evidence. Distinguish facts supplied by the user from hypotheses and value judgments. Historical claims require a checked source when precision matters; contemporary applications below are our adaptations. A lens supplies no authority to take external actions.

Scale the method to the stakes. For a simple request, use only the move that changes the answer. If the method adds no useful distinction, say so and complete the task directly. End with a usable answer or next action, not philosophy vocabulary alone.
