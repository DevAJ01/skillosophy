# Routing catalog

| Skill | Task obstacle | Useful output | Main failure risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| [philosopher-socrates](../../philosopher-socrates/SKILL.md) | A disputed definition or unsupported assumption | Counterexample and revised claim | Repeated questioning without a deliverable |
| [philosopher-kant](../../philosopher-kant/SKILL.md) | A rule involving honesty, coercion, duty, or agency | Maxim, universalization, agency-preserving alternative | Confusing bad consequences with contradiction |
| [philosopher-nietzsche](../../philosopher-nietzsche/SKILL.md) | An inherited ideal or status norm needs examination | Competing explanations, revaluation, experiment | Unsupported motive attribution |
| [philosopher-epictetus](../../philosopher-epictetus/SKILL.md) | A setback or outcome outside complete control | Agency map, responsible action, contingency | Passivity or blaming people for constraints |
| [philosopher-popper](../../philosopher-popper/SKILL.md) | An empirical hypothesis needs a real test | Discriminating prediction and failure criterion | Post hoc rescue or noisy results treated as proof |
| [philosopher-wittgenstein](../../philosopher-wittgenstein/SKILL.md) | People use the same word differently | Concrete uses and acceptance criteria | Dismissing substantive disagreement as wordplay |
| [philosopher-rawls](../../philosopher-rawls/SKILL.md) | Institutional allocation or unequal opportunity | Rules compared by liberties, opportunity, disadvantage | Aggregate benefit mistaken for fairness |
| [philosopher-mill](../../philosopher-mill/SKILL.md) | Consequences of alternatives must be compared | Stakeholder harms, uncertainty, reversal condition | Invented welfare numbers or majority-only accounting |
| [philosopher-aristotle](../../philosopher-aristotle/SKILL.md) | Habits, incentives, or professional judgment matter | Purpose, context, practice, feedback | Arithmetic compromise presented as virtue |
| [philosopher-descartes](../../philosopher-descartes/SKILL.md) | A complex argument rests on doubtful premises | Assumption ledger and reconstructed conclusion | Demanding certainty before any action |

For conflicts among lenses, load [philosopher-council](../../philosopher-council/SKILL.md). Recommendations express fit, not measured performance.
